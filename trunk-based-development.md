# trunk-based development

Quotes from [this hackernews discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=36571253):

> Branches vs. on-trunk misses the mark, IMO: the question is what's your practices like for how frequently changes get integrated. There's multiple dimensions of freedom in how you do things.
>
> You can do trunk based development badly where your mainline is always broken, and you can do feature branching badly where you sit on a branch for months and then break the world when you merge.

End of the day trying to integrate often and keep mainline green.

We do code review before merge to main because async distributed team.
